Insurance

Coverage is active and current: $100,000 motor truck cargo, $1,000,000 auto liability, and $1,000,000 commercial general liability with a $2,000,000 aggregate. A certificate of insurance is available on request.

Weight Capacity & Entry Clearance

26FT box with a hydraulic liftgate, 8,800 lbs weight limit, 2,500 lbs liftgate capacity, and 94" x 94" door clearance. Pallet jacks are on board for ground level drops.

Booking

Same day service is available when the lane allows, but scheduling ahead gets you a confirmed slot. Runs beyond the CSRA, up to 200 miles from Augusta, should be scheduled in advance.

Driver Assist

Hand loading/unloading and inside delivery are available when arranged in advance, so it's built into the quote. If your pickup or drop has no dock and no forklift, say so when you book.
